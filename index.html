02 — Research

What I've built in the lab

Biosignal AI · USC Khan Lab / CHLA · IEEE TBME / MRM (in prep)

ECG Reconstruction During MRI Scanning

Built a BiLSTM + transfer learning pipeline to reconstruct 12-lead ECG from a wearable patch device during low-field MRI — a hard problem because MRI introduces severe electromagnetic noise that corrupts cardiac signals. Cross-functional collaboration with electrical and RF engineers at CHLA. Manuscript in preparation.

SynapNet-Edge — Edge Model Compression

Hybrid SSM-attention-memory architecture with component-aware joint quantization (2-bit SSM QAT, 4-bit attention via SmoothQuant + AWQ) and a learned BAEE memory-eviction policy. 4.4x compression with no accuracy loss.
